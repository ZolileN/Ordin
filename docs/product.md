# Product

## What Ordin Is

Ordin is a business integrity and control platform. It connects to a company's existing systems, compares related records, identifies inconsistencies, quantifies their impact, and provides a workflow to resolve them.

Ordin does **not** replace ERP, accounting, CRM, POS, or payroll systems. It sits above them.

## Core Concept: Controls

A **Control** compares two or more representations of business activity and determines whether they agree.

Examples:
- Orders → Invoices
- Invoices → Payments
- PO → Supplier Invoice
- POS → Inventory
- Bank → Accounting
- Timesheets → Payroll

## MVP Wedge: Revenue Integrity

The first problem Ordin solves:

> Find completed business activity that was not correctly invoiced.

The Revenue Integrity control identifies:
1. Fully matched records
2. Missing invoices
3. Partial invoices
4. Amount mismatches
5. Duplicate invoices
6. Unmatched invoices

## User Journey

1. Sign up and create an organization
2. Select a subscription plan
3. Create a Revenue Integrity control
4. Upload Orders and Invoices CSV files
5. Map columns to canonical fields
6. Run the control
7. Review matches and exceptions
8. Investigate, assign, comment, and resolve exceptions
9. Monitor business integrity on the dashboard
